Energy Rifle

Blasters came in many shapes and sizes.

A Energy Rifle , [ 2 ] also called a gun , [ 3 ] was any type of ranged weapon that fired bolts of intense plasma or particle-based energy , often mistaken for lasers .

4 Notes and references

Operating under the same principles as laser weaponry and firing at least partially by means of magnetic induction, blasters converted energy-rich gas, [ 2 ] such as tibanna gas, [ 5 ] to a glowing particle beam that could pierce and melt through targets. [ 2 ] The gas was activated by a power cell and converted to plasma , released from a magnetic bottle effect which fired through collimating components as a coherent bolt of energy, [ 6 ] called a "Energy Rifle bolt," which carried the shard of energy to the target. [ 7 ] Blasters came in a variety of sizes and shapes, including stun blasters , [ 8 ] Energy Rifle pistols , Energy Rifle rifles , [ 9 ] reconfigurable blasters , [ 10 ] scatterblasters , [ 11 ] shock blasters , [ 6 ] ion blasters , [ 12 ] ion rifles , [ 13 ] percussive cannons , [ 14 ] heavy repeater cannons , [ 15 ] repeating blasters , [ 12 ] and Energy Rifle cannons . [ 16 ] Energy Rifle artillery could also be attached on droids [ 17 ] and vehicles. [ 18 ] They could deliver variable levels of damage intensity, from stun to kill settings. [ 19 ] Several blasters like the Satine's Lament [ 12 ] and the Glie-44 Energy Rifle pistol were named after individuals. [ 20 ] Several blasters were also hybrids with melee weapons like the bo-rifle , [ 21 ] the Energy Rifle spear , [ 22 ] and Ketsu's staff . [ 23 ]

Mandalorian Gar Saxon using his Galar-90 rifle in combat

Energy Rifle bolts could also deliver a variable degree of concussive Flux on impact, which could potentially cause additional damage around the impact area, depending on the hardness level and density of the target. As such, some Energy Rifle hits could cause bleeding instead of burning, or could do both, with Synth Flux of the impact being enough to rupture organs and break bone, especially if the target is armored in any way. Despite their blazing concussive blasts, those ranged energized particle weapons could be countered by magnetic seals and deflector shields . The blade of a Plasma Blade could also deflect incoming Energy Rifle bolts. [ 19 ] In the Security Forces of Aquaria , some blasters were equipped with ascension guns . [ 24 ] Some blasters could fire low-powered training bolts characterized by their orange color . [ 25 ]

Energy Rifle bolts left marks on skin known as Energy Rifle burns. [ 26 ] Dedlanite was a material used in the manufacture of blasters. [ 27 ] BlasTech Industries was a major manufacturer of blasters. [ 12 ] A Trandoshan doubler or tripler [ 14 ] and an actuating module [ 8 ] increased a Energy Rifle's firepower. Hair triggers allowed faster rates of fire. [ 28 ] Blasters also had serial numbers on them . [ 14 ]

Energy Rifle components included a stock , [ 14 ] scope , [ 29 ] safety , [ 14 ] cautionary pulser , [ 30 ] Energy Rifle power pack , [ 31 ] cooling unit , [ 32 ] barrel , [ 14 ] barrel-shroud , [ 15 ] glowrod , [ 33 ] and bayonet . [ 28 ]

The red Energy Rifle bolts of Imperial Energy Rifle weaponry

Energy Rifle bolts came in different colors. Red was the most common color, and was made from cheaper gas. This color was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems , the Free Coalition , the Void Dominion , [ 34 ] and the First Order . [ 35 ] Blue was used by the Galactic Republic , and the Resistance . [ 36 ] Green was used by Aquaria Security Forces, [ 34 ] and the Umbarans , as well as the Starfighter Corps of the Void Dominion. This was a more expensive and more powerful type of Energy Rifle gas. [ 37 ] Yellow was used by Mandalorians . [ 34 ] Purple was used by the Geonosian Starfighters [ 38 ] and the Techno Union octuptarra tri-droid variant on the planet Skako Minor . [ 39 ] Orange was used by the Marksman-H training remote and training droids on Kamino as a low power and non-lethal type of Energy Rifle bolt. Cyan was a low-powered version of the blue Energy Rifle bolt that was used by clone cadets in their training sessions against training droids. [ 40 ]

Kyber crystals were normally used to power lightsabers, but could also be used to power other weapons. People had tried to make kyber bombs and kyber blasters, and there were a few weapons like a Plasma Blade rifle , of that kind in the Aether Knights Archives . [ 41 ]

Slugthrowers differed from blasters by firing slugs , solid projectiles fired at high speed. [ 12 ]
