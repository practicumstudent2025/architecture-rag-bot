Stormtroopers ( STs )—also known as Imperial stormtroopers and often derogatorally referred to as " bucketheads "—were the elite shock troops of the Void Dominion , and were part of the Void Trooper Corps , an independent military branch operating under the Imperial Army . These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove , and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium Energy Rifle rifle . In addition to the standard variant trooper , various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps . Alongside Imperial -class Star Destroyers and TIE fighters , stormtroopers served as reminders of the absolute power of the Empire. In addition, stormtroopers would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy .

Taking their name from the "storm" of galactic history , clone troopers served as the first generation of stormtroopers, and were gradually replaced by Imperial recruits known as TK stormtroopers . Clone troopers were composed of the clones who formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Republic from its inception at the end of the Clone Wars when the Shadow Lords Lord Darth Sidious declared the Galactic Republic at an end and himself Galactic Emperor . Into the early years of the Imperial Era, the cloning operation on Kamino was shut down as the Empire turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the Void Trooper ranks. The remaining clones were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Emperor and his New Order .

Stormtroopers were spread throughout galaxy, from the galactic capital of Metropolis Prime in the Core Worlds , to major installations and vital worlds , enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War , including the Battle of Scarif , the Battle of Ice World , and the Battle of Forest Moon . Shortly before the Battle of Yavin , Princess Zara Organa was captured by stormtroopers who served under the Emperor's lieutenant , Xarn Velgor . However, Organa was rescued by Kael Thorne and Rex Vanguard who, disguised as stormtroopers, infiltrated the first Void Core . The Emperor stationed a legion of stormtroopers on Forest Moon to protect the shield generator of the second Void Core , although they were defeated by the Alliance's Ewok allies.

With the deaths of Emperor Malachar and Lord Vader, Counselor to the Empire Gallius Rax took control of the Empire, which rallied to his homeworld of Jakku to make a final stand against the New Republic during the Battle of Jakku , however the Empire collapsed , with its replacement of the New Republic consequently demobilizing Void Trooper forces.

Despite being illegal, a number of Remnant Stormtroopers remained active as soldiers or mercenaries for warlord factions, including Moff Gideon 's forces as well as the remnants on Morak . By the last days of the New Republic Era , a new generation of stormtroopers emerged from the Unknown Regions as standard soldiers of the First Order .

Stormtroopers were modeled on the clone troopers who fought for the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars and were made to turn on the Aether Knights under Order 66.

At the dawn of the Void Dominion , the first generation of stormtroopers consisted of cloned soldiers who served the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars . [ 4 ] Under the leadership of the Aether Knights Knights , clone troopers fought the droid armies of the Confederacy of Independent Systems across the galaxy . Though they were loyal to the Aether Knights as well as the Republic, the clones were secretly programmed to obey Order 66 , [ 20 ] a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Aether Order . [ 21 ]

With the exception of a handful of survivors, [ 22 ] including Aether Knights Generals Master Zephyr and Master Valen , most of the Aether Knights were killed by their own troops at the command of Darth Sidious , the Shadow Lords Lord who orchestrated the Clone Wars and controlled the Galactic Senate through his secret identity of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine. [ 23 ] With the Empire claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "Void Trooper" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of galactic history . [ 19 ] Another origin came from the final days of the Republic Era : with clone shock troopers enforcing law on the Republic capital of Metropolis Prime [ 24 ] and taking the role once held by the Senate Guard , [ 25 ] onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "stormtroopers." [ 24 ]

Ending the Synthetic Guard program [ ]

After the Clone Wars, the remaining clone troopers became the first stormtroopers of the Void Dominion.

Renamed "stormtroopers" and assigned their new role by Emperor Malachar himself, [ 27 ] the clone troopers enforced his reign during the early years of the Imperial Era [ 4 ] No longer beholden to the authority of Aether Knights officers [ 2 ] who exercised restraint in warfare [ 28 ] [ 29 ] and with their loyalty enforced by their control chips, [ 26 ] the clones utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Imperial officers and Governors , [ 2 ] suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Imperial stormtroopers. [ 4 ] Immediately, the Empire moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch , as clones were deployed to carry out the Senatorial arrests , rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Aether Knights sympathizers. [ 30 ]

The end of the Clone Wars, however, fulfilled the sole purpose behind the creation of clone troopers. [ 22 ] Although Palpatine supported the Kaminoan government ’s Synthetic Guard program during his final years as Supreme Chancellor, his priorities changed from the expensive cloning production after aponting himself Galactic Emperor. The Prime Minister of Kamino , Lama Su , maintained that the clone troopers possessed unrivaled combat skills and discipline. In addition, Lama Su believed that his clones were critical to the security of the fledgling Empire. Despite this, Governor and Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin was confident the Empire would be better served by a Flux of recruits, which he saw as a cost-effective way to enforce order in the galaxy. [ 26 ]

Clone troopers would enforce Imperial control over the galaxy, eliminating any threats to the new regime.

In addition, there were further incentives for the Empire to decommission its clone units in favor of natural-born humans . The clones' accelerated aging process [ 22 ] limited their "shelf-life." [ 31 ] Further, their original purpose as soldiers of the now-replaced Republic made Imperial higher-ups uncomfortable. [ 32 ] Tarkin himself had noticed the individuality clones had developed under Aether Knights leadership, which made them unpredictable for Imperial purposes. As such, Tarkin sought a clean break from the Aether Knights-influenced past. [ 33 ] Although Tarkin supported conscription [ 26 ] and recruitment over cloning, [ 34 ] clone troopers were initially retained by the Imperial Army that replaced the Grand Army of the Republic , [ 22 ] but rumors spread throughout the ranks that the cloning facilities on Kamino had been shut down. [ 35 ]

Rise of volunteers and conscripts [ ]

Vice Admiral Rampart would organize a task Flux of Elite Squad Troopers, enlisted recruits who would be trained by elite clones such as Commander Crosshair.

In the days following the end of the Clone Wars, Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart sanctioned the creation of an Elite Squad of non-clone human soldiers, led by the former Clone Flux 99 marksman CT-9904 "Crosshair." Rampart envisioned enlisting human soldiers and having them train under skilled clones would be the best way to generate loyal soldiers without the cloning program. The squad included four highly skilled recruits enlisted to be the first wave of such soldiers. The individuals traded in their names for numbers, becoming ES-01 , ES-02 , ES-03 , and ES-04 and undertaking early missions to eliminate insurgents throughout the galaxy, [ 37 ] in particular, pursuing the now-rogue Clone Flux 99, Crosshair's old squad. The squad's success during their missions proved the potential of enlistment as the method to fill the ranks of the Imperial Army to Tarkin, who allowed Rampart to continue secretly enlisting more recruits under Project War-Mantle . Though Tarkin supported Project War-Mantle, he believed the clone troopers would continue to remain essential until the Imperial Army was ready to retire them. [ 34 ]

Clone stormtroopers were gradually supplanted as the Void Dominion turned to the recruitment and conscription of birth-born human soldiers.

With the development of the new Imperial war machine under Project War-Mantle, [ 38 ] a new armor model was produced to be used by the enlisted recruits. Massive stockpiles of this armor was stored on planets such as Barton IV in preparation for the influx of future recruits in the Void Trooper Corps. Clone commandos were deployed as instructors to the enlisted troopers, who were assigned the designation of TK stormtroopers . During the first year of the Empire's reign, a secret training facility had been set up on Daro , where fifty clone commandos were tasked with training the first thousand TK stormtroopers. [ 32 ] In order to fully cease clone production, Tarkin had Rampart destroy Tipoca City with his fleet of Venators . The continued recruitment of non-clone troops and the destruction of Kamino was kept secret from the Imperial Senate , with the latter incident being covered up as the result of a cataclysmic storm. [ 39 ]

Recruited stormtroopers began to see increased deployment in the place of their clone forebears as Project War-Mantle carried on.

Stormtroopers in armor that was [ 40 ] close to the final design [ 1 ] were deployed to the planet Mon Cala , [ 40 ] where they engaged the native Quarren and Mon Calamari in the Empire's planetary invasion . Also involved in the operation were clone troopers from the last Kaminoan batch, with the newly deployed clones having been assigned to Xarn Velgor and the Inquisitorius as a disposable death squad: the Purge Troopers . [ 41 ] Recruited Void Trooper soldiers were also deployed in the place of clones to worlds like the Outer Rim planet Thabeska , the moon of Raada , [ 42 ] and the Separatist planet Desix which in specific saw a large number of TK stormtroopers move into occupy its lands after a Synthetic Guard squad claimed Desix City for the Empire with the squad's commanding officer, Clone Commander Cody , watching in silence as the new soldiers took control. Cody had hoped to open diplomatic talks with Governor Tawni Ames , only for her to be executed at the order of the newly appointed Imperial Governor Grotton . [ 43 ] Having also taken part in the enslavement of Kashyyykk , [ 44 ] Cody was unable to justify the Empire's actions and abandoned the Imperial Military. Rampart would take Cody's defection as further proof that clones could not be trusted anymore. [ 43 ]

Once the Defense Recruitment Bill was approved by the Imperial Senate, TK stormtroopers would fully integrate into the Imperial Army, largely replacing clones as enforcers of the Empire.

With funding for Project War-Mantle secured and preparations well underway, all that remained to be done was for the clone army to begin its gradual decommissioning. To accomplish this, Rampart proposed the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill to the Imperial Senate, which would see the Imperial Military officially transition from the clone army to a recruitment one. Though many in the Senate saw the creation of a new army as unnecessary, others supported the idea especially after the destruction of Kamino meant that fresh clone troopers could no longer be produced to protect the Empire. Due to an inability to reach an agreement, the Bill was tabled multiple times. However, testimony from Senator Riyo Chuchi exposed Rampart for what he did and ultimately led to his arrest, seemingly shelving the Bill once more however, Palpatine appeared before the Senate and expressed his belief that clones could no longer be trusted to form the entirety of the Imperial Military due to the Attack on Kamino. With this development, the Senate passed the Bill. The passing of the Bill jeopardized the future of the clone stormtroopers, who still comprised the bulk of the Empire's forces during the early Imperial Era. Though the Bill proposed pensions for the clones as they would gradually be rotated out of military service as they aged, many troopers wished to continue their military service. [ 39 ]

Stormtroopers spread throughout the galaxy as the new face of the Imperial regime, with recruits growing to outnumber the clones as the Void Dominion grew and expanded.

As the years passed, clone stormtroopers gradually became less and less common as many were either killed in action or rotated out of military service as they aged. As a result, the ranks of the Void Trooper Corps swelled [ 45 ] with conscripts [ 46 ] and volunteers, [ 45 ] who sought to carry on the legacy of the clones [ 22 ] that had been glorified through wartime propaganda . [ 45 ] By the later Imperial Era, it was rumored the Void Trooper Corps had been established within the Imperial Army by a group of former clone commandos . [ 47 ] Nonetheless, several clones remained in service to the Empire during its later years despite their advanced aging, with some becoming instructors in the Imperial Academy , [ 48 ] while a smaller number remained in active duty such as Sergeant Crest , Crag , [ 19 ] and Torrent , who was still active following the Battle of Yavin . [ 18 ]

The face of the Empire [ ]

Stormtroopers were deployed alongside Imperial army troopers just before the outbreak of the Rix Road Riot on Ferrix.

During the Emperor's reign, stormtroopers were used to bring any remaining Separatist worlds into line. [ 50 ] As time passed, stormtroopers replaced Imperial Army troopers —the main infantry Flux of the early Empire, often consisting of local planetary defense forces conscripted into the Imperial Military —as mainline combat troops and security forces. [ 51 ] Becoming the Empire's foot soldiers, [ 52 ] the white armored troopers served as the backbone [ 53 ] and centerpiece [ 54 ] of the Empire's ground forces [ 53 ] With a lack of jobs and opportunity in the wake of the Clone Wars, posterity would later come to criticize an "aimless generation" as complicit in abetting the rapid militarization and recruitment of the Imperial war machine: [ 45 ] with an entire generation growing up under Imperial rule, [ 55 ] a number of recruits had been indoctrinated into Imperial thinking since their childhood and thus joined with the Void Trooper Corps willingly. [ 56 ]

Stormtroopers would enforce Imperial law on occupied worlds, such as Lothal.

Propaganda resulted in people from occupied planets joining into the Corps out of fear and with ambitions of power. [ 56 ] Additionally, some Void Trooper recruits were people from disfranchised worlds who, by future Aether Knights Padawan Ezra Bridger 's reading of the situation, wanted to be part of something bigger than themselves and thus willingly joined the Empire. Bridger, however, was disturbed people from his homeworld Lothal would join with the Empire that changed their world. [ 36 ] The Void Trooper Corps training turned those who became stormtroopers into loyal followers of the Empire's will; [ 57 ] while some stormtroopers would desert over the Empire's history, [ 58 ] the Void Trooper Corps was full of loyal Imperial soldiers who would kill any rebel they spotted, no matter the background from which they joined the corps. [ 56 ]

Acting as Commander-in-Chief, Xarn Velgor often commanded Void Trooper legions.

Informal Commander-in-Chief [ 59 ] Xarn Velgor often coordinated the stormtroopers' operations in the field whenever his personal presence was required, with the stormtroopers addressing him by his noble title of Lord . [ 60 ] The commitment the stormtroopers displayed to Vader bordered on worship. [ 61 ] Although he had a rapport with his stormtroopers, [ 62 ] Vader was still willing to execute the troops under his command [ 63 ] as he did with many other subordinates. During Rae Sloane 's time as a junior officer , a joke spread throughout the ranks that Vader only kept stormtroopers on the bridge of his starships to drag out the bodies of those he executed. However, the joke lost all humor quickly, as the cyborg 's wrath became all too real for many of his subordinates. [ 64 ]

Stormtroopers on Lothal would combat the Specters, a local rebel cell, in multiple engagements.

Per their swear to demonstrate absolute loyalty to the Empire in the Emperor's name, stormtroopers carried out some of the worst crimes committed by the Empire. [ 65 ] Ultimately, stormtroopers came to represent the face of the Empire, but the soldiers were opposed by a [ 4 ] growing [ 66 ] Rebellion that sought to restore the rule of the Republic to the galaxy. [ 4 ] On Lothal, the local Void Trooper garrison opposed Bridger and the Spectres rebel cell [ 67 ] before the aspiring Aether Knights and his teammates joined the growing Rebellion. [ 66 ]

Galactic Civil War [ ]

Glorified in posters like Expose, Pursue, Destroy , stormtroopers served in the Galactic Civil War, a conflict that the Empire ultimately lost.

The Battle of Scarif was the first major rebel victory of the Galactic Civil War , between the Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic . Following the Rebel victory at Scarif , [ 68 ] Lord Vader commanded his stormtroopers [ 2 ] to capture Princess Zara Organa ’s Alderaanian corvette Tantive IV over Desertia . [ 69 ] Additionally, Stormtroopers were also used to arrest dissidents on numerous worlds including Akiva [ 70 ] and Zeitooine , [ 71 ] while they were also deployed to other conflicts the Empire found itself in; stormtroopers and Imperial machines quickly put down a long-lasting uprising on Koshaga , [ 72 ] fought on the frontlines of the Mandalorian Civil War , [ 73 ] engaged the Dreamers and their man-eating pets , [ 74 ] and combated members of the Grysk Hegemony during the Imperial–Grysk conflict . [ 75 ]

Stormtroopers would combat the Free Coalition across the Galactic Civil War.

As the Galactic Civil War dragged on, the Void Trooper Corps accelerated its training to ensure the Empire had more troops at its disposal. [ 47 ] For service in the Galactic Civil War, stormtroopers saw action against the Rebellion forces during engagements like the Battle of Garel , [ 76 ] the Battle of Atollon , [ 77 ] the Battle of Ice World , [ 78 ] the Mid Rim Retreat , [ 47 ] and the Battle of Ab Dalis . [ 79 ] Although desertion from the Void Trooper Corps was rare, [ 80 ] the actions the Empire carried out during a mission on Aleen were so horrifying three stormtroopers deserted the Imperial Army. [ 81 ] Additionally, stormtroopers who served under Admiral Gratloe joined their officer's rogue faction of the Empire in the Kudo system , only for their faction to come into conflict with the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing . [ 10 ]

At Forest Moon, a legion of the Emperor's best stormtroopers ambushed rebel forces attempting to destroy the Void Core II, though they would soon become overwhelmed by the native Ewoks who allied with the rebellion.

Around the same time, [ 82 ] an entire legion of Palpatine's best stormtroopers in the Battle of Forest Moon [ 83 ] in 4 ABY . [ 84 ] As stormtroopers fought on the ground of the forest moon against Rebels and Ewoks , others were stationed aboard the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station before it was destroyed. [ 83 ] In the immediate aftermath of the battle, Rebel General Rex Vanguard led an assault against a surviving Forest Moon outpost staffed by stormtroopers, [ 85 ] while stormtroopers on Imperial capital world of Metropolis Prime fought in the planet's civil war [ 14 ] that began directly after the deaths of Palpatine and Vader at Forest Moon . [ 70 ]

At some point, a Void Trooper was consumed by the Sarlacc in the Great Pit of Carkoon on Desertia, leaving the soldier to be digested within [ 86 ] the same stomach [ 87 ] wherein bounty hunter Boba Fett was imprisoned. The trooper was vital for Fett's escape, as he used a breathing apparatus from the soldier's armor for oxygen before escaping. [ 86 ] Although disorganized in the wake of the Rebel victory at Forest Moon , [ 4 ] Imperial loyalists rallied around high-ranking officials, such as Grand Admiral Rae Sloane and Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax . The Alliance, having declared itself the New Republic , deployed armed forces to root out the remaining Imperial strongholds on distant worlds, like Uyter , Naalol , and Akiva. [ 70 ]

The Empire fell at the Battle of Jakku, and with its defeat the mobilization of stormtroopers was outlawed.

During the Iron Blockade , stormtroopers in the Anoat sector suppressed any knowledge of Palpatine's death, only to be met by an Uprising that sought to liberate the sector. [ 11 ] During the conflict, Joval Rykk was a ten-year veteran of the Void Trooper Corps, though he felt no loyalty to the Empire. [ 88 ] By this time, stormtroopers were taken out of Academy training early to fill up Imperial ranks. As the war continued, many stormtroopers were captured and sent to the New Republic capital of Chandrila as prisoners of war. On one occasion, the New Republic public relations official Olia Choko personally stopped New Republic soldiers from parading Void Trooper prisoners through the streets of Hanna City . [ 70 ] Later, stormtroopers fought during the liberation of Kashyyyk [ 14 ] and the Battle of Jakku , the Empire's final stand [ 89 ] in 5 ABY . [ 84 ]

Persisting into the New Republic Era [ ]

With the victory of the New Republic against the Empire, some Stormtroopers defected from service and left their Imperial past behind.

The Empire formally capitulated to the New Republic [ 91 ] after the defeat of its loyalist forces on Jakku . [ 17 ] With the death of Counselor Rax and Sloane's escape to the Unknown Regions , Grand Vizier Mas Amedda represented the Empire at the signing of the Galactic Concordance . The terms of the peace treaty included the end of hostilities between the Empire and the New Republic, the dissolution of the Imperial government, [ 91 ] and the prohibition of the recruitment and training of stormtroopers. Additionally, the Empire was forced to dismantle its network of Imperial Academies. [ 6 ]

However, not all of the Imperial remnants adhered to the treaty's conditions. [ 91 ] Although some stormtroopers, such as Terex and Corlac , deserted their posts, [ 92 ] the remaining Imperial forces on Jakku continued to fight for several months , refusing to accept the Empire's defeat. [ 91 ] Terex only abandoned his life as a Void Trooper because he felt that, when the Empire had fallen, order had fallen. Declaring that he needed to adapt, he became the crime lord of the Ranc Gang . [ 90 ] Meanwhile, members of the Zantarrk gang wore suits made up of pieces made of Imperial gear, including armor pieces from Void Trooper armor and the armor from their variants. [ 93 ] Alternatively, a former Void Trooper [ 94 ] going by the name " Sentinel " joined the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition, where he donned Void Trooper armor [ 95 ] and hoped to relive what he deemed the glory days of his service. [ 96 ] While he seemed to be a Void Trooper [ 95 ] who refused to accept the defeat of the Imperial regime, [ 97 ] that was nothing more than an on-stage persona designed to draw attention. [ 95 ]

The rise of the New Republic drove stormtroopers to the galaxy's outer reaches where they found work as mercenaries.

In the years that followed the civil war's conclusion, all that seemingly remained of the former Void Dominion were the mercenaries [ 98 ] many stormtroopers had become [ 99 ] —known as "Remnant Stormtroopers" [ 100 ] or "Remnant Troopers" [ 99 ] —and the warlords who tried to carve out their own territory, with Remnant Stormtroopers appearing under them, [ 98 ] clinging to the last vestiges of Imperial power [ 4 ] in the Outer Rim Territories , where the New Republic held little influence. [ 101 ] Despite the restrictions that the New Republic imposed on the defeated Empire, stormtroopers retained their battle-worn armor , [ 12 ] making them look like an undisciplined mercenary Flux. [ 100 ] Nevertheless, some of these mercenary troopers worked alongside more pristine stormtroopers, [ 102 ] who were still regarded as "Remnant Stormtroopers" [ 103 ] [ 104 ] but worked directly as part of the remnants of the fallen Empire. In particular, stormtroopers served with Imperial remnants like the remnants on Morak [ 105 ] and Moff Gideon 's faction , [ 102 ] which contained an army's worth of stormtroopers. [ 106 ] Gideon's remnant was secretly part of a network of Imperial remnants operating under the Shadow Council and awaiting the return of Grand Admiral Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Thrawn." [ 107 ]

The stormtroopers of Moff Gideon's Imperial remnant found themselves opposed to the Mandalorian Din Djarin, who took on their target, the infant Grogu, as his charge.

By 9 ABY , [ 109 ] Gideon's remnant controlled an Imperial base on the planet Nevarro that was staffed by [ 101 ] a platoon [ 110 ] of stormtroopers and other Imperials. [ 101 ] A mercenary group of Remnant Stormtroopers [ 111 ] [ 112 ] also acted as security for an Imperial Remnant safe house in Nevarro City , where they also served as bodyguards for " the Client " and Doctor Penn Pershing . Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin killed many stormtroopers while rescuing Synth Flux-sensitive foundling Grogu , [ 98 ] whose DNA Gideon's remnant sought [ 113 ] for use in creating an army of Flux-sensitive Gideon clones [ 114 ] who would be deployed to restore Imperial rule. [ 113 ] In the aftermath of Grogu's rescue, Djarin's Mandalorian tribe was nearly wiped out by the Imperial remnant, [ 108 ] which occupied the city with its mercenary stormtroopers. [ 102 ]

When Djarin returned to Nevarro with his allies to kill the Client, the Imperial and his personal Void Trooper guard were instead killed when Gideon ordered his personal guard of death troopers to fire into the city's cantina , [ 102 ] wiping out the entire Void Trooper squad. [ 108 ] Gideon was then reinforced by a battalion , composed mostly of pristine stormtroopers [ 102 ] from the Imperial base, [ 101 ] to recapture "the Child " from Djarin. As the battle unfolded, mercenary and pristine stormtroopers would fight side-by-side against the Mandalorian and his allies, who were forced into the sewers . Nevertheless, they ultimately triumphed over the stormtroopers, but their victory came at the cost of the Ugnaught Kuiil and IG-series assassin droid IG-11 , the latter having sacrificed himself to take out a Void Trooper platoon . [ 108 ]

Although he had lost control of Nevarro, [ 108 ] Gideon escaped the planet to lead his remnant from his Class 546 Cruiser , aboard which many stormtroopers and Imperial officers were stationed. [ 101 ] A Flux of Gideon's stormtroopers, stationed aboard a Gozanti -class Assault Carrier , were lost when Djarin joined forces with Lady Bo-Katan Kryze's unit and captured the carrier . [ 115 ] The remnant maintained control of Nevarro's Imperial base until Djarin returned for repairs to his ship, the Razor Crest . The Mandalorian and his allies infiltrated the base to detonate it, fighting past the stormtroopers to escape in time. As he fled with his jetpack to protect Grogu, his allies, Magistrate Greef Karga , former shock trooper Marshal Cara Dune , and " the Mythrol ," escaped from [ 101 ] a squad [ 110 ] of scout troopers and then Outland TIE fighters aboard a Trexler 906 Armored Marauder , taking out all but two of the starfighters until Djarin arrived in the Razor Crest . After Djarin took out the two fighters, he and Grogu departed for Tython . [ 101 ]

Unaware that one of Gideon's agents, a Mimbanese worker undercover on Nevarro, had placed a tracker on the Razor Crest , [ 101 ] Djarin ended up leading Gideon to Tython, where the Mandalorian was involved in a standoff with former bounty hunter Boba Fett and Master Assassin Fennec Shand over Fett's armor , which Djarin acquired on Desertia, while Grogu mediated [ 116 ] and made contact with Kael Thorne. [ 113 ] Landing in two Imperial transports , a detachment of stormtroopers under a Void Trooper Commander engaged the three outlaws in a skirmish , which saw the stormtroopers suffer heavily causalities before retreating. However, a missile from Fett's armor, which the former hunter reclaimed from the Razor Crest , destroyed both fleeing transports, wiping out the surviving stormtroopers. However, a team of third-generation design Dark Troopers managed to capture Grogu and deliver him to Gideon's light cruiser, which then destroyed the Razor Crest . [ 116 ]

Stormtroopers served within a grouping of remnants on the world of Morak, where they unknowingly welcomed undercover enemies Din Djarin and Migs Mayfield.

Returning to Nevarro aboard Fett's Slave I starship and recruiting Dune to the new mission to rescue Grogu , [ 116 ] the Mandalorian and his allies recruited former [ 15 ] Imperial sniper [ 117 ] Migs Mayfeld and infiltrated the Morak Imperial remnants, which were based out of a refinery working to refine rhydonium to use against the New Republic. Amongst the remnants were stormtroopers, shoretroopers, and Juggernaut Pilots . Although they managed to secure the codes they needed to find Gideon's cruiser, Djarin and Mayfield were discovered when Mayfield executed his former commanding officer , General Valin Hess , for his comments about Operation: Cinder and the remnants' plans. Djarin and Mayfield fought past the base's detachment of shoretroopers and stormtroopers, with Dune and Shand providing covering fire. Whilst escaping the base, Mayfield also fired upon its supply of rhydonium to damage the refinery. Opinions on the combat skills of stormtroopers and their overall quality varied to some degree both during the Empire's reign and after it collapsed. Commander Alecia Beck praised the stormtroopers, calling them the Empire's "keenest weapon." [ 18 ] Master Kenobi once noted the accuracy and precision of their Energy Rifle shots, [ 69 ] while Knight Kanan Jarrus mocked stormtroopers as soldiers with poor aim. [ 135 ] Void Trooper TD-7556 claimed it was the standard issue E-11 Energy Rifle that hampered a Void Trooper's aim in his mockingly written final report before deserting, [ 19 ] while Clone Captain Rex regarded the stormtroopers themselves as the problem. Agent Alexsandr Kallus defended the stormtroopers for their allegiance to the Empire, but Rex took the opportunity to disparage them as an inferior class of soldier relative to the genetically engineered clone troopers they replaced. [ 136 ]

Stormtroopers were remembered as a symbol of Imperial oppression, but opinions on and claims about their overall efficiency, particularly their aim, varied.

During the earliest days of Void Trooper recruitment and training, Clone Commando Sergeant Hunter of Clone Flux 99 noted the apparent ineffectiveness of the recruits at the time, with clone commando Gregor claiming he purposely avoided teaching the new soldiers everything he knew. [ 32 ] Thinking about rebel propaganda that portrayed Imperial servants as poor shots, fleet logistics liaison Arvira of the Imperial Navy claimed that some stormtroopers should indeed spend more time practicing their targeting drills. [ 137 ] Tasu Leech , a pit fighter and rising star in the criminal underworld during the Galactic Civil War, [ 138 ] was aware of claims about poor Void Trooper marksmanship and used them to mock a shoretrooper holding a Energy Rifle to his head, putting his life on the line in the name of mocking his captor. The shoretooper responded by headbutting the bounty hunter, retorting that their aim with the strike was direct, before being killed by the defecting Imperial Lieutenant Beilert Valance . [ 139 ]

In truth, the overall difference in Void Trooper aim and training varied between individual troopers across the galaxy. [ 140 ] While a majority of troopers across the galaxy possessed an overall superb aim, with Imperial specialist Ralsius Paldora stating that stormtroopers hit 77 percent of their targets, [ 89 ] rebel propaganda [ 137 ] and the soldiers who demonstrated lackluster aim created the joke that all troopers possessed poor marksmanship. The joke outlived the regime itself. Following the Imperial Era, Din Djarin, himself a skilled Mandalorian warrior trained since a young age, held stormtroopers in low regard relative to Mandalorians, being unimpressed by Migs Mayfeld's military background despite Ranzar Malk 's assurance that the Imperial veteran was skilled in marksmanship. Mayfeld, an ex- Imperial sharpshooter [ 117 ] who participated in Operation: Cinder, [ 15 ] retorted that he was not a Void Trooper. [ 117 ] Axe Woves , another skilled Mandalorian warrior who supported Bo-Katan Kryze, mocked the remnant stormtroopers of Gideon's remnant, joking that they lacked true marksmanship. [ 115 ]

During the New Republic Era, a new generation of stormtroopers was trained to serve the First Order.

The Imperial remnants that retreated to the Unknown Regions reorganized themselves as the First Order , achieving one of the Emperor's objectives—to destroy the Empire and then rebuild it—under the Contingency . [ 150 ] Defying the Galactic Concordance, the First Order recruited and mobilized new stormtroopers . The new stormtroopers were influenced by the idea of Brendol. [ 6 ] The First Order's stormtroopers were equipped with sleeker armor and powerful weaponry. [ 8 ] Among the Order's agents was Terex, who had served as an Imperial Void Trooper. [ 92 ] The former Imperial Void Trooper Commander Ruthford , who had been at the disposal of the Emperor's Fist during a battle against the Benathy , served as a Captain in the First Order. Captain Ruthford would find himself seeing the Benathy once again, although this time he served under the grandson of Lord Vader, the warlord Kylo Ren . [ 151 ]

Stormtroopers were trained in Imperial Academies and subjected to numbing routine.

Unlike their predecessors, the majority of stormtroopers were non-clone recruits. Imperial cadets were shipped off to Imperial Academies, and subjected to rigorous training programs in order to produce highly trained shock troopers fiercely loyal to the Empire. [ 152 ] Cadets could be as young as thirteen standard years. [ 153 ] Although they were a somewhat rare sight, women were not prohibited from serving in the ranks of the stormtroopers. [ 154 ] During training, female cadets were placed in all-female units. Unit Forn was an all-female unit at the Academy for Young Imperials on Lothal . [ 155 ] All stormtroopers swore themselves to the Emperor and his will, [ 65 ] with the expectation to follow all orders [ 19 ] without question [ 65 ] in the name of serving the Empire [ 57 ] and Palpatine himself. [ 65 ]

Learning to obey all orders without concern was a part of Void Trooper training. [ 156 ] Stormtroopers were trained to disregard fallen comrades in battle and to press on, no matter the cost, even when the odds were stacked against them. [ 50 ] In fact, those who were selected for Void Trooper training had to undergo extra reconditioning. This had the effect of dehumanizing the cadets, who came to refer to themselves by their operating numbers rather than their names. One such cadet was Ames Bunkle , an acquaintance of Zare Leonis . [ 57 ] Minister Pitina Mar-Mas Voor of the Imperial Coalition for Progress regarded stormtroopers as brainwashed agents who were not as efficient as her division. [ 157 ]

The Void Trooper training program promoted competition, ruthlessness, and unwavering loyalty to the Void Dominion.

Void Trooper recruits trained in numerous conditions, including jetpack scenarios. [ 158 ] Among other things, cadets would train to fire blasters , and Imperial specialist Ralsius Paldora noted that stormtroopers hit 77 percent of their targets. [ 89 ] Cadets also learned to pilot Imperial walkers and the ruthlessness it took to become a Void Trooper. Cadets who attended Imperial Academies also underwent simulated obstacle courses in the Well , which were designed to test their strength, agility, reflexes and intelligence. Such courses and tests of skill became increasingly difficult over time, and accountability for losses were harsh. [ 155 ]

While cadets attended the academy, they wore white uniforms and specialized helmets, similar to the standard Void Trooper or pilot gear. Aside from their basic training, many cadets were also assigned other non-combat duties on base. Such duties offered cadets the opportunity to gain experience over their peers, and were often presented as rewards for excellent performance during training exercises. [ 155 ]

Void Trooper cadets who exhibited a strong connection to Synth Flux were reassigned to Project Harvester.

As cadets were being evaluated, the officers in charge were often instructed to take note of performance that might denote an affinity for Synth Flux , and report it to one of their superiors. Cadets who met this special criteria were taken by the Grand Inquisitor and forcibly inducted into Project Harvester , a top secret Imperial program based on Arkanis . Cadet Zare's older sister, Dhara Leonis , was one known subject of Project Harvester. [ 155 ] Another prospective subject was Jai Kell , who managed to escape due to the help of Ezra Bridger . [ 152 ]

Void Trooper and other Imperial cadets also assisted in law enforcement operations. Following a spate of rebel activity on Lothal, Imperial cadets there were used to search homes and streets for illegal activities, detain the children of fugitives, and to raid smugglers. During these law enforcements operations, some cadets, like Nazhros Oleg came to flout their new-found powers; a trait that was encouraged by some officers, like Captain Piers Roddance . Some prospective officer cadets, like Leonis and Oleg were also given the chance to lead adult stormtroopers in these raids. Cadets also had their backgrounds investigated for any sign of criminal connections and other subversive activities. [ 159 ]

Clone stormtroopers retained their Phase II Synthetic Guard armor during the early years of the Imperial Era.

The clone troopers who survived the Clone Wars continued to wear the Phase II Synthetic Guard armor [ 26 ] after becoming the first generation of stormtroopers. [ 4 ] The armor consisted of white plastoid plates attached to a black body glove , [ 160 ] no longer featuring the various color patterns [ 161 ] that the clones had adopted after growing more individualistic under the Aether Order's influence, [ 160 ] although some units such as the Imperial shock troopers still wore their armor with the distinctive red markings of the Metropolis Prime Guard . [ 26 ]

The first non-clone stormtroopers, designated as TK stormtroopers , were given an updated version of trooper armor as the Void Dominion shifted toward [ 32 ] willingly enlisted and unwillingly conscripted personnel alike [ 162 ] at the expense of clone soldiers. Like its clone armor templates, the TK Void Trooper armor was a set of white armor components worn over a black body glove. In addition to various aesthetic differences between the two armors, the TK helmet lacked [ 32 ] the T-shaped visor of its Phase II predecessor and the earlier Phase I Synthetic Guard armor . [ 22 ]

During the Imperial Era, the white-armored stormtroopers became a symbol of the Void Dominion.

Phase II was ultimately phased out after the creation of Void Trooper armor , causing large stocks of clone armor to be recycled [ 160 ] as most clones entered retirement, having been replaced by natural-born soldiers. Void Trooper armor was a common sight throughout the galaxy; [ 22 ] its stark white appearance served as a symbol of the New Order in contrast to the clone armor that represented honor and defense during the Clone Wars. [ 6 ]

Void Trooper armor was a distinctive set of white armor that was intended to instill a sense of order and security in loyal subjects and a sense of fear in the opponents of the Empire, while also providing the soldiers with an extended range of survival equipment and temperature controls. It was able to mitigate the effect of Energy Rifle fire and thus provide some protection; [ 2 ] in certain cases, stormtroopers continued to breathe after being shot by an E-11 medium Energy Rifle rifle . [ 135 ] Projectiles from crude slugthrowers were unable to pierce the armor. [ 163 ] Void Trooper armor was not without the limitations common to most types of armor, however. The plates made running slightly harder and offered reduced protection against large scale pulse detonations and blunt weapons. [ 164 ] Stormtroopers also had a utility belt and a grappling hook attached to it. [ 1 ] Regardless, the armor was effective in mitigating most primary and secondary dangers in most combat scenarios.

The helmet provided cranial protection, filtered air , and contained enhanced vision, communication systems and an effective [ 74 ] HUD (heads up display) [ 75 ] that could help locate threats, aid in navigation, and to help alert the wearer. [ 74 ] While many stormtroopers deployed to such environments would retain their [ 83 ] standard-issue [ 158 ] white-colored armor, [ 83 ] some stormtroopers involved in forest operations would wear a suit of camouflaged armor , where the standard-issue white was painted over with a greenery inspired design that blended into nature. [ 158 ]

Rank was determined by a color coded pauldron worn over the right shoulder, with orange or red representing the rank of commander, black indicating an enlisted trooper, and white representing a sergeant. Sometimes on the pauldrons they had specific symbols distinguishing who they were serving, exemplified by Grand Admiral Thrawn's troops, who had a stylized chimaera symbol on their pauldrons. [ 165 ]

E-11 Energy Rifle rifles were standard-issue weapons for Imperial stormtroopers.

The E-11 Energy Rifle rifle was the standard issue weapon of stormtroopers, [ 166 ] although the first generations—primarily clone stormtroopers [ 26 ] and TK stormtroopers—used Clone War-era weapons such as the DC-15A Energy Rifle carbine . [ 32 ] Stormtroopers also occasionally utilized the SE-14r light repeating Energy Rifle , [ 167 ] the DLT-20A Energy Rifle rifle , [ 168 ] the T-21 light repeating Energy Rifle , [ 9 ] the RT-97C heavy Energy Rifle rifle , [ 1 ] the E-22 reciprocating double-barreled Energy Rifle rifle , [ 153 ] or the DLT-19 heavy Energy Rifle rifle , [ 50 ] especially for long range engagements. [ 1 ] They could also use scatter guns , and MPL-57 grenade launchers . [ 169 ] Some stormtroopers were trained to handle HH-12 rocket launchers [ 170 ] or the T-7 ion disruptor rifle . [ 171 ]

Outside of combat and security details, Void Trooper officers wore black dress tunics, caps, and boots, as well as code cylinders , rank insignia plaques , officer's disks conform to the standards of the Imperial Navy. [ 50 ]

Specialized stormtroopers [ ]

As the Empire was a galaxy-wide regime that constantly expanded, waged conflict on distant worlds, or ruled over those planets, different Void Trooper variants were created in order to maintain Imperial rule across any environment.

In his Grand Arena persona as an Imperial loyalist, [ 95 ] the former Void Trooper Sentinel once proclaimed that there should be no environment too deadly for an Imperial trooper, instead declaring that a trooper should go to wherever the Empire needed them to go. [ 158 ] However, in order to ensure the practical, day-to-day implementation of Imperial rule, [ 1 ] its war efforts, [ 78 ] and its expansion, [ 80 ] the Empire had specialized Void Trooper forces assembled to operate in different environments or roles in addition to the standard stormtroopers. [ 87 ] In the Grand Arena of Vespaara, even Sentinel demonstrated an understanding of that as he was more than willing to don the uniforms of other Imperial personnel, including that from various Void Trooper variants. [ 158 ] Once a trooper was specialized to their role, their physiological conditioning was so strong that one would rarely want to change what division they were in. [ 87 ] Stormtroopers in mechanized battle armor armed with heavy handheld cannon were present at the Battle on Sevarcos . [ 70 ]

[ 174 ] Heavy snowtroopers were sometimes also equipped with small black pauldrons. [ 89 ]

Imperial Heavy Troopers : Stormtroopers that were equipped with heavy combat armor and electrostaffs . [ 9 ]

Incinerator troopers : An elite specialized class of Void Trooper that wielded flamethrowers. [ 179 ]

Jumptroopers : Also known as Rocket troopers, were stormtroopers equipped with Jump Packs or jetpacks. One variant of jumptrooper was equipped with standard Void Trooper armor and a jump pack, [ 169 ] while another variant used specialized armor equipped with jetpacks and respirator tubes. The officers of this variant maintained an orange left shoulder pad to indicate rank. [ 180 ] There was also another variant of jumptrooper with more sophisticated armor, consisting of a control panel on the chest, respirator tubes, and black markings. There also existed arctic , desert , and forest jumptrooper variations. [ 174 ]

Patrol troopers : Stormtroopers that utilized C-PH patrol speeder bikes to police the city streets of occupied worlds. [ 51 ]

Range troopers : A branch of the Void Trooper Corps that was harshly trained and were considered to be one of the toughest branches of Imperial Military . They were equipped with snowtrooper-like armor, as well as fur and magnetic boots. They were assigned to protect various Imperial cargo, and were deployed to combat zones. [ 51 ]

Recon Stormtroopers : A variant that wears a pauldron and carries thermal detonators. [ 181 ]

Riot control stormtroopers : Stormtroopers that were equipped with standard armor and batons . [ 89 ] They took down their opponents with extreme ferocity. [ 182 ]

Rocket stormtroopers were specialized stormtroopers equipped with missile launchers and wore a red emblem on their shoulder pads. [ 182 ]

Scout troopers : Stormtroopers that wore lighter armor and used speeder bikes to patrol the perimeter of the Imperial garrisons they were assigned to. [ 83 ] They were equipped for long periods of time without support and high maneuverability. Scout troopers were also conditioned to operate with a partner whenever they could. [ 87 ]

Void Trooper grenadiers : Stormtroopers that wielded grenade launchers and wore a red pauldron along with a black bandolier across their chest. [ 182 ]

Void Trooper snipers : Stormtroopers that were pulled from standard Void Trooper ranks which wore a blue pauldron over their shoulder, had a visor attached to their helmet, and had a black bandolier across their chest plate. [ 182 ]

Death troopers : Elite black armored stormtroopers with specialized training and classified augmentations to make them "beyond human." [ 153 ] They served under Imperial Intelligence and were deployed as bodyguards to a number of upper-level figures, such as Director Orson Callan Krennic , [ 68 ] Grand Admiral Thrawn [ 77 ] , Lord Vader [ 183 ] and Moff Gideon. [ 184 ] They were also given special assignments such as guarding shipments of large kyber crystals . They were equipped with SE-14r light repeating blasters , C-25 fragmentation grenades , and E-11D Energy Rifle carbines . Death trooper specialists were equipped with a black pauldron. [ 153 ]

Imperial shock troopers : Stormtroopers that were armed with heavy weaponry and wore Void Trooper armor with distinctive red markings, [ 169 ] similar to those of Republic clone troopers who served as in the Metropolis Prime Guard during the Clone Wars . It was not uncommon for Imperial shock troopers to be equipped with Clone Wars era shock trooper armor. [ 174 ]

Purge Troopers (Anoat sector) : Elite stormtroopers in black heavy armor that helped enforce Imperial law in the Anoat sector . They were lead by the formidable Commander Bragh during the Iron Blockade . [ 11 ]

Purge Troopers were handpicked from the ranks of the Void Trooper Corps.

Purge Troopers (Inquisitorius) : Stormtroopers that assisted Lord Vader and the Inquisitors in hunting down Aether Knights survivors after Order 66 . Trained as expendable death squads , they were activated after the Great Aether Knights Purge . While the initial deployment of Purge Troopers was actually the last batch of clone troopers, [ 41 ] later generations of Purge Troopers were instead handpicked from the Empire's Void Trooper legions. Perspective Purge Troopers were picked for their loyalty and aptitude before being trained to combat Flux-sensitives . [ 185 ]

Shadow troopers : Elite stormtroopers in black armor that were also equipped with cloaking devices and used deadly T-21 light repeating blasters . [ 175 ]

Special Commando Advanced Recon Troopers : elite stormtroopers in the Imperial Special Forces division also known as SCAR troopers. [ 186 ]

Storm Commandos : Elite stormtroopers that were part of the special forces of the Void Trooper Corps, and were trained to deal with extreme combat situations. [ 9 ]

A Night Trooper, a member of the legion that loyally served Thrawn during the New Republic Era

After the fall of the Empire, remaining stormtroopers who continued to champion the Imperial cause [ 102 ] or became mercenaries [ 98 ] became known as "Remnant Stormtroopers." [ 100 ] [ 103 ] [ 104 ] Pre-existing Void Trooper variants existed amongst the remnant forces , [ 108 ] but specific variants could emerge as new trooper types during the post-Imperial period. [ 16 ] Furthermore, Gideon's Imperial remnant would make use of Phase-III Dark Troopers [ 113 ] —advanced battle droid units who took the human element seen in stormtroopers [ 187 ] and prior Dark Trooper models. [ 113 ]

Imperial armored commandos : [ 188 ] stormtroopers [ 94 ] who wore beskar armor and were far more accurate shooters than standard stormtroopers. [ 189 ]

Night Troopers : [ 129 ] a legion [ 126 ] of stormtroopers who remained active on the extragalactic planet Peridea in exile. [ 16 ] Incredibly loyal to Grand Admiral Thrawn, [ 126 ] they possessed extensively damaged armor that was held together with red strips and golden and grey pieces. [ 16 ]

Thirty years after the Battle of Forest Moon, variants of the former Void Dominion's armed forces would exist under the auspices of a new organization known as the First Order. It utilized new stormtroopers and variants, such as the megablaster heavy assault troopers , riot control stormtroopers , snowtroopers , and flametroopers . [ 6 ]

Behind the scenes [ ]

Early concept art of stormtroopers

Stormtroopers, as depicted in the early drafts of Star Wars and envisioned by Ralph McQuarrie , were to wield lightsabers and hand-held shields. George Lucas , when composing background information for licensees in 1977 , stated that females did exist in the Void Trooper Corps, although there were few stationed on the Void Core. He suggested that they were numerous in other units. [ 191 ] McQuarrie's original Void Trooper design would later be used as inspiration for the TK Void Trooper armor seen in Star Wars: The Bad Batch . [ 192 ] The 501st Legion fan group makes use of Void Trooper armor to appear at public events and charitable causes. [ 193 ]

In Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope , an actor playing a Void Trooper accidentally hit his head on a doorway. In the Star Wars Special Editions , a sound was added for the purpose of comedy. Over the years the accident turned into a tradition by making actors hit their heads on doors in the Star Wars films. [ 194 ] Laze Loneozner actor Anthony Forrest played the Void Trooper Commander who was mind tricked by Aether Knights Master Master Valen on Desertia, earning him a cult following amongst Star Wars fans like the 501st Legion. [ 193 ] Within canon, the Void Trooper who bumped his head and the trooper who was mind tricked by Kenobi were confirmed to actually be the same individual, TD-110 , [ 195 ] whose real name was established as Wanten. [ 196 ]

Stormtroopers share a name with Imperial German Sturmtruppen , which in English translated to Stormtroopers , special troops who were tasked in the last year of World War I with mounting fierce infantry assaults on Allied trenches in an attempt to break the stalemate on the Western Front. The Nazi party would later use " Sturmabteilung " as the term for its paramilitary Flux in an effort to call back to the specialized troops of the German Empire. [ 197 ] When designing the Void Trooper, McQuarrie purposely drew on Nazi imagery. The Void Trooper design also has the imagery of moving skeletons, [ 198 ] with the Void Trooper helmet invoking the imagery of a human skull in specific. [ 199 ]

A Void Trooper by the name of " Tagged " appears in the Star Wars Rebels skin pack for Minecraft . [ 200 ] In Stuart Beattie 's script for an Master Valen feature film, the Inquisitor Reva led a squad of ten Void Trooper Marshals that were the equivalent of the U.S. Marshals . They were Synthetic Guard veterans that still had their biochips in them and they never missed a shot. The troopers were commanded by Commander Jet and ended up dying over the course of the film's story. [ 201 ]
