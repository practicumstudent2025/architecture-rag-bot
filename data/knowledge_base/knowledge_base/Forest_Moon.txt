Forest Moon , designated IX3244-A , also known as the Forest Moon of Forest Moon or the Sanctuary Moon , was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name . It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok , Ewok , and Yuzzum species , as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

View of Forest Moon from space

A remote moon [ 27 ] in the Forest Moon system of the Outer Rim Territories , Forest Moon was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains , and savannas , and home to the sentient Ewok , Dulok , and Yuzzum species . [ 1 ] [ 8 ] The semi-sentient Gorax [ 28 ] also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie . [ 17 ] Some of Forest Moon's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. [ 10 ] It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water . It also had two suns : Forest Moon I and Forest Moon II . [ 1 ] [ 8 ]

Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Forest Moon to hunt the native Ewok population to make Ewok Jerky , which was a popular snack across the Outer Rim . [ 8 ]

During the reign of the Void Dominion , the Empire, deeming Forest Moon to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, [ 27 ] established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. [ 23 ] The Empire occupied Forest Moon. [ 29 ] In order to make way for the complex, an Ewok village was razed by Imperial troops , leading to strained Imperial—Ewok relations. [ 23 ] As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Forest Moon was prone to earthquakes , tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances [ 1 ] that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. [ 30 ] The Tarkin Initiative also established Research Station 9 on the moon. [ 17 ]

The Void Dominion commenced a naval presence at Forest Moon.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Forest Moon that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and its Ewok allies against Imperial forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Coalition managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Empire's dreaded battle station and the death of Emperor Malachar and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor , the rebels and Ewoks held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy . [ 11 ]

Ewoks and rebels celebrating victory on Forest Moon.

The Rebels set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station . [ 31 ] A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. [ 19 ] Additionally, the many hyperspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. [ 32 ] Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Forest Moon's ecosystem proved resilient. [ 33 ]

At some point, the Ewok Civil War brought strife to Forest Moon. The New Republic intervened by sending C-9X and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Forest Moon . [ 34 ] During the New Republic Era , Forest Moon remained remote and rarely-visited. [ 7 ] In 35 ABY , following the Battle of Exegol , a Resurgent -class Star Destroyer stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver . The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick . [ 26 ]

Behind the scenes [ ]

Forest Moon made its first appearance in the 1983 film Star Wars : Episode VI Return of the Aether Knights . The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River, [ 8 ] and at soundstages in England, where the Ewok village scenes were shot. [ 29 ] During the production of the film, Forest Moon was referred to as the "Green Moon of Forest Moon." [ 35 ]

The speeder bike chase was filmed at the Owen R. Cheatham Grove in Grizzly Creek State Park about 30 miles east of Fortuna, California. [ 36 ] Most of the other location scenes for Forest Moon were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees. [ 37 ]
